## 1. Brand Identity & Name Clearance
- **Brand Name**: **Cortelo** (pronounced *kor-TEL-oh*)
- **Tagline**: *"Systems of Intelligence."*
- **Origin**: From *cortex* (brain/neural intelligence) and *architectural structure*.
- **Trademark Search Results**: 
  - Comprehensive clearance performed across software, AI, and IT agency classifications (Class 9 & 42).
  - No active trademarks or conflicting AI/software consultancies found under the name "Cortelo" (unlike *Voxlyn* or *Nuvexa* which have existing operating entities).
- **Positioning**: An independent, elite studio engineering bespoke AI systems, autonomous multi-agent clusters, and resilient full-stack platforms from model to interface.
- **Founding Leadership**:
  - **Founder & Lead AI Architect**: **Hunzila Nisar** (Autonomous Agents, PyTorch, Neural Pipelines, Latency Optimization)
  - **Co-Founder & Head of Systems**: **Mahreen Hashir** (Deterministic Evaluation, Guardrails, MLOps, Enterprise Security ASVS L2)

## 3. Visual & Aesthetic Architecture (Fragment.im Benchmark)
- **Primary Palette**:
  - Obsidian Base: `#07090E`
  - Glass Surface: `rgba(15, 19, 29, 0.75)` with 20px backdrop blur
  - Signal Mint: `#2DD4A7`
  - Crisp Paper Text: `#F2F0EA`
  - Muted Label Text: `#8C94A6`
  - Hairline Border: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`
- **Aesthetic Rules**:
  - Zero "vibe-coded" cheap clichés (no random glowing green dot matrices, no fake purple-to-blue gradients).
  - Immersive 3D interactive centerpieces (refractive glass polyhedra).
  - Horizontal sliding project reels with verified telemetry metrics.
  - Floating glassmorphic header with live timezone coordinate indicators (PKT / UTC+5).
  - Slide-out project commissioning drawer.
